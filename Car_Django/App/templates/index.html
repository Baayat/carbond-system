Description

A web-based Car Bond Management System designed to manage the car inventory, specifications, customers, employees, car sales and sale payments efficiently.

System Modules

  • Brand Module — Manage vehicle brands used in the showroom inventory
  • Model Module — Manage model names, years, and their associated brand
  • Car Module — Manage cars, their status, mileage, price and engine type (Car, Electric and ICE car)
  • Specification Module — Record car specifications such as doors and their values
  • Customer Module — Register customers and their contact details
  • Employee Module — Register employees, roles and hire dates
  • Sale Module — Record sales linking a car, an employee and a customer
  • Payment Module — Record payments made against a sale and calculate the balance

Management Workflow

  1. Register the car and its specification
  2. Register the customer and the employee handling the sale
  3. Create a sale linking the car, employee and customer
  4. Record payments against the sale and calculate the balance
  5. Check when a car is due for service based on its mileage
